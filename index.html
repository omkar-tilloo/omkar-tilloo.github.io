Who I Am

I'm a Supply Chain & Operations professional pursuing my Master of Engineering Management at NC State University. With a foundation in Mechanical Engineering and hands-on industry experience, I specialize in designing efficient supply chains, optimizing warehouse operations, and managing complex cross-functional projects from end to end.

My approach is data-first: whether that means integrating ERP workflows to eliminate inventory discrepancies, building optimization models to minimize operational cost, or translating raw operational data into Power BI dashboards that drive better decisions at the leadership level.

I've led procurement coordination, reduced lead times, applied Lean methodologies on the warehouse floor, and managed international humanitarian logistics — giving me a 360° view of how supply chains and operations actually work under pressure.

Outside the classroom, I serve as IEEE TEMS Chair at NC State, bridging the gap between students and industry leaders in engineering management and supply chain.
